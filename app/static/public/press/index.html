01 · Logo marks

The mīm-trace — four canonical variants.

The mark is a circle (lens / scale weight / Arabic letter mīm) connected by a chart-line path to an ascending dot. Always reproduce with adequate clear-space — minimum padding equal to the height of the circle on all four sides.

04 · Usage

Six rules that preserve the brand.

The brand earns its premium feel through discipline, not decoration. These six examples cover the deviations we see most often in submitted assets.

Do
Use gold-on-warm-dark. The canonical pairing. Adequate clear-space all around. The mark always faces upward.
Do
Use dark-on-light for print. Inverse the mark to charcoal-on-paper for institutional documents and statements.
Do
mizanquant
ميزان كوانت
Pair DM Sans + Cairo for bilingual. EN above, AR below. Always show both together on bilingual surfaces.
Don't
Don't retint the mark. Only gold (#c8963e), charcoal (#0c0c10), or white are valid. No purples, blues, or gradients.
Don't
mizanquant
— elevate your portfolio 🚀
Don't add taglines, glows, or emoji. The brand is institutional, not consumer. No marketing flourishes, drop shadows, or 🚀 / 💰 anywhere.
Don't
NEW 2026!
Don't crowd the mark. Reserve clear-space equal to the height of the ring on all sides. Never abut promo banners or stickers.

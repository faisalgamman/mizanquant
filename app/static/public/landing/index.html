Architecture

A five-layer loop, supervised by a standing risk desk.

Every trade begins as a halal-screened candidate and ends as a stress-tested position with a Kelly-sized risk budget. The loop runs end-to-end six times a day, with the risk desk standing alongside it as a continuous guard.

01 / GATE
Halal screener
AAOIFI four-screen gate. Debt · interest income · cash & receivables · haram industries. Re-scanned on a 24h cycle against FMP fundamentals.
02 / SIGNAL
Forecast lab
14 models — transformer · BiGRU seq2seq · dilated CNN · vanilla / 2-path RNNs · classical ensemble — vote per symbol, per session.
03 / CONSENSUS
AI consensus
Model votes pass through a confidence-weighted aggregator. STRONG BUY requires both score ≥ 90 and ≥ 11 of 14 BUY votes.
04 / SIZE
Kelly allocator
Continuous Kelly with sample-size shrinkage and a hard 25% ceiling. Half-Kelly default. Static budget overrides Kelly when conservative.
05 / EXECUTE
Risk desk · execute
Pre-trade VaR check, guard pass, kill-switch arm, broker handoff (Alpaca / IBKR), fill watcher, Telegram alert, daily report.

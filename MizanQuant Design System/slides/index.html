Architecture

A five-layer loop, supervised by a standing risk desk.

Every trade begins as a halal-screened candidate and ends as a stress-tested position with a Kelly-sized risk budget. The loop runs end-to-end six times a day.

01 / GATE
Halal screener
AAOIFI four-screen gate. Debt · interest · cash · haram industries. Re-scanned daily.
02 / SIGNAL
Forecast lab
14 forecast models across transformer, RNN, CNN and classical families vote per session.
03 / CONSENSUS
AI consensus
Confidence-weighted aggregator. STRONG BUY needs ≥ 11 of 14 votes plus score ≥ 90.
04 / SIZE
Kelly allocator
Continuous Kelly · sample-size shrinkage · half-Kelly default · 25% hard ceiling.
05 / EXECUTE
Risk desk · execute
Pre-trade VaR · guard pass · kill-switch · broker handoff (Alpaca / IBKR) · audit log.

04 / 06
Risk controls

Risk is mathematical, not editorial.

Position size is governed by the continuous-Kelly criterion. Every order passes a VaR / correlation / liquidity / kill-switch gate before it reaches the broker.

Continuous Kelly · position sizing

For a strategy with per-trade returns r, the Kelly-optimal fraction maximizes long-run growth. We use half-Kelly by default — roughly 75% of full-Kelly growth with ~50% of the drawdown.

f* = mean(r) / var(r)

Sample-size shrinkage applies when n < 30. Static risk budget overrides Kelly when more conservative. A hard 25% ceiling is never crossed.

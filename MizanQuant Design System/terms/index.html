01Fee schedule · standard institutional terms
Custom MFN terms available · $25M+ mandates
Term Headline Mechanism
Management feeرسم الإدارة 2.00% pa Charged quarterly in arrears on the average daily net asset value of the partner's sleeve. Pro-rated for partial quarters. Net of all platform infrastructure costs.
Performance feeرسم الأداء 20% NNP Twenty percent of net new profits above the high-water mark, crystallised annually on 31-December. Computed in USD, net of management fees. NNP = NAVend − max(HWM, NAVstart) − mgmt
High-water markحد العلامة المائية Perpetual A perpetual high-water mark per partner — no reset on losses. The performance fee only applies to NAV above the all-time peak NAV (less prior fee accruals). Loss-recovery quarters carry zero performance fee.
Hurdle rateمعدل العائد المطلوب 4.00% pa Soft hurdle, indexed to the 3-month US Treasury yield (currently 4.18%) with a 4.00% floor. Once the hurdle is cleared, the 20% performance fee applies to the entire NNP — not just the excess.
Subscription windowنافذة الاكتتاب Monthly Subscriptions accepted on the first business day of each calendar month with 5 business days' notice. Pre-funding required by close of business one day prior. Minimum subscription: $1,000,000.
